# Connectify - Business Process Plan

This document details the business model, commercial strategy, monetization logic, and regulatory frameworks of the **Connectify** platform.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

Connectify is a new paradigm in social platforms. Unlike traditional networks that optimize for screen time, vanity metrics, and ad revenue, Connectify is structured to support **mental wellness, authentic boundaries, and real-world personal growth**.

### Our Pillars:
- **Trust Over Reach**: Features like *Trust Circles* ensure users only share what is relevant to appropriate audiences, minimizing social anxiety and identity dispersion.
- **Mental Wellness**: Built-in wellbeing settings (Like-Free Mode, Slow Feed, Screen Limits) prevent dopamine addiction and doomscrolling.
- **Goal accountability**: Replacing passive timelines with actionable progress tracking.
- **Decentralized Reputation**: Trust ratings based on constructive contributions rather than passive follower counts.

---

## 2. Customer Segments & Competitor Analysis

### Customer Segments:
1. **Mindful Netizens**: Users who are fatigued by mainstream social feeds but seek digital communities.
2. **Growth Seekers**: Individuals actively trying to achieve personal, academic, or professional milestones through accountability.
3. **Privacy Advocates**: Users who demand full boundary control (via Trust Circles and Disposable Profiles).

### Competitor Mapping:
- **Instagram / TikTok**: Optimized for infinite scrolling, dopamine loops, and vanity. *Connectify contrast*: Hides likes, enforces feed slow-down, sets break reminders.
- **Reddit**: Offers community-based anonymous posting but lacks verification and connection. *Connectify contrast*: Integrates verified expertise, reputation, and disposable profiles tied directly to communities.
- **LinkedIn**: Focuses on professional networks but promotes career hyper-inflations. *Connectify contrast*: Standardizes verified expertise and peer-reviewed growth milestones.

## 3. Monetization Strategy

Connectify relies on two main revenue streams that align with user value:

### 3.1 Premium Subscriptions (Connectify Premium)
Processed through our checkout flows (integrated with Paymongo), subscriptions offer users extra capabilities without compromise:
- **Tier 1: Free Tier**
  - Standard social features (feed, circles, goals).
  - Basic wellbeing tools.
  - Limit: Up to 2 active disposable profiles.
- **Tier 2: Premium Tier (199 PHP/mo or equivalent)**
  - Enhanced wellbeing settings (custom limit profiles, premium slow-feed caps).
  - Unlimited disposable profiles.
  - Verified Expert Badge (upon verification of credentials).
  - Monthly allowance of 100 Peace Coins.
  - Profile customization (Premium badge and borders).

### 3.2 Micro-Transactions (Peace Coins 🪙)
Peace Coins are utility-only virtual tokens (not real-money tradeable post-purchase) used to reward contributions:
- **Acquiring Coins**: Purchased in batches via credit card.
- **Tipping Content**: Users can tip posts or constructive comments to reward high-value insights.
- **Reputation Flow**: Tipping moves a portion of the tipping value into the recipient's reputation score, driving visibility in the explore tab.

## 4. Legal & Regulatory Compliance Framework

Connectify is built with privacy-first legal architectures, establishing full compliance frameworks:

### 4.1 GDPR & Data Protection (Right to be Forgotten)
- **Data Portability**: Users can download their complete profile data from Settings.
- **Scrub Policy**: When an account is deleted, the backend performs hard deletes of all personal Mongoose documents.
- **Consent Flags**: Consent to cookies and terms is explicitly logged at sign-up and stored with timezone offsets.

### 4.2 COPPA & Safety
- **Age Restriction**: Enforces minimum age limit of 13 years at registration.
- **Verification Systems**: Disposable profiles do not bypass age controls, as they are anchored to verified parent accounts.

### 4.3 Digital Legacy & Trustee Probate
Connectify introduces the **Digital Legacy Protocol** to manage profile estate rights legally:
- **Inactivity Trigger**: Set to 180 days by default. After 180 days of zero login events, the trustee is contacted.
- **Action Execution**:
  - *Memorialize*: Accounts freeze, title changes to "Memorialized," and comments are closed.
  - *Delete*: System triggers recursive deletes on posts, goals, and profiles.
  - *Transfer*: Trustees receive a unique reset code to claim the login details.
